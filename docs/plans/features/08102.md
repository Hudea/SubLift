## 字段与状态合同（冻结）

### BatchTaskStatus（String Codable CaseIterable）

`waiting / preparing / extracting / exporting / completed / failed / cancelled / interrupted / skipped`

合法转换（`canTransition(from:to:)` 纯状态机）：

| from \ to | preparing | extracting | exporting | completed | failed | cancelled | interrupted | skipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| waiting | ✓ | — | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| preparing | — | ✓ | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| extracting | — | — | ✓ | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| exporting | — | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| completed/failed/cancelled/interrupted/skipped | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | —（终态） |

### BatchTask（Identifiable Equatable Codable Sendable）

- `id: UUID`、`sourceURL: URL`（标准化由 Scanner 负责）、`configuration: ExtractionConfiguration`
- `outputURL: URL?`（输出计划由 08104 填充）、`status: BatchTaskStatus`
- `progress: Double?`（0–1，clamp）、`result: BatchTaskResult?`（completed 后：entryCount/outputURL/runtimeIdentity）
- `failureMessage: String?`、`createdAt: Date`
- **`runToken: UUID?` 不进入持久化值**（CodingKeys 排除；decode 后为 nil）
- `make(...)`：创建时经 `EngineCapability.normalizedSelection` 归一化（隐藏 Mock 最终归一化入口）
- `replaceConfiguration(_:)`：仅 waiting 可替换（显式 intent）；preparing 及以后拒绝
- `transition(to:)`：非法转换返回 false 不改变状态

### BatchQueueState（Codable Equatable Sendable）

- `status: BatchQueueStatus`（idle/running/paused/interrupted）
- `tasks: [BatchTask]`、`runningTaskID: UUID?`
